# Gameplay HUD and display controls

Press the backquote (`) key during gameplay to show or hide the ECS and scene tools. It also releases mouse capture so the panels can be used. Their individual visibility, position, and collapsed state are kept between toggles. The inspection HUD remains independent; press Q to inspect the weapon and player status.

Open the pause menu with Escape to choose 16:9, 16:10, 21:9 ultrawide, or a custom aspect ratio. The resolution arrows step through sizes in the selected family, from smaller to larger, filtered by GPU dimension and render-pixel limits. Custom ratios keep their original proportions across steps, with dimensions rounded to whole pixels. Enter width and height and choose **Apply custom** for any other size; this also sets its custom ratio. The 21:9 family includes standard marketed ultrawide resolutions, whose exact ratios can differ slightly. The **Native** button selects the browser viewport resolution and updates the displayed aspect ratio to match it.

Viewport scaling cycles between **Fill** (crop to cover), **Fit** (show the whole scene with bars), and **Pixel perfect** (nearest-neighbor whole-pixel replication). Pixel-perfect chooses the largest integer scale that fits. If the render is larger than the viewport, it displays a centered 1× crop and warns you to select a lower resolution. UI stays sharp at viewport resolution. A separate checkbox controls whether the performance panel appears during regular gameplay. The performance panel is also available when the ECS tools are shown. Display and performance preferences are kept in browser local storage, and invalid or unsupported resolutions are rejected before they reach the renderer.

The pause menu is a scene of ECS UI elements. `menu_navigation.lua` emits validated-setting requests, the browser host applies device limits and persistence, and the accepted state returns to the menu. `editor_overlay.lua` owns the gameplay visibility behavior and emits `hud.editor_visibility_changed` through the Lua event system.

The pause overview shows the selected level's local preview and the current scene's loaded splats, LOD, chunk count, elapsed playtime, display settings, and player bindings. Choose a section in the left rail to change HUD preferences, display, movement and mouse controls, draw distance and sky, or the scene's sound-effects layer. The control, graphics, and audio changes are dispatched to the gameplay Lua scripts and their ECS components. The game currently has one top-level destination, so both **Choose level** and **Return to menu** open the level selector.
