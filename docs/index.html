The vision

The world is already
full of worlds.

CounterSplat explores what happens when the texture and character of real places meet the systems of a game.

Our goal is a flexible foundation for moving through captured spaces, shaping interactions, and making experiences that feel grounded in a place.

From scan to scene

A WORLD IN MOTION.

Photographic detail is only the beginning. The engine brings the pieces together in real time.

01

Capture the place

Gaussian splats represent a scanned environment as a field of tiny, view-dependent points.

SOG / Gaussian splats
02

Build the scene

Chunks stream as you move. An ECS connects the camera, UI, audio, and world into a scene.

Streaming / ECS / WebGPU
03

Make it playable

Lua scripts add controls, collision, weapons, and game modes on top of reusable engine systems.

Lua / physics / gameplay

Built with Rust, Lua, WebAssembly, and WebGPU. The browser demo is an evolving prototype.

Inspired by PlayCanvas: its open-source engine and SuperSplat showed what Gaussian splats can do on the web, and CounterSplat's splat shaders and voxel collision build on their MIT-licensed work.
